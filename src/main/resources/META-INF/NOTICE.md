* Native Java engine, equipment domain, monster catalog, integration utilities, icon and
  inherited tests: https://github.com/mcdalcin/better-dps-calculator/tree/3fd61bb
  (downloaded September 5, 2026), GPL-3.0.
* Combat reference and the inherited 150 deterministic fixtures: OSRS Wiki DPS Calculator,
  https://github.com/weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc/tree/b6bc098dc0d742b2b763375d2e78e1b611a22070
  (July 9, 2026), GPL-3.0. Fixtures/equipment domain were generated by the upstream native
  project's sync workspace; this workspace did not regenerate them against the latest Wiki.
* Spell catalog, weapon-category style table, prayer ordering/icons, tab/slot/stat icons and current-source comparison:
  https://github.com/weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc/tree/89c3e25 (downloaded September 5, 2026).
* Local optimizer spell levels and autocast compatibility use factual tables from OSRS
  Wiki contributors (reviewed September 5, 2026):
  https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Standard_spellbook ,
  https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Ancient_Magicks ,
  https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Arceuus_spellbook and
  https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Autocast . These augment the existing spell catalog;
  they do not verify account quest unlocks, rune supplies or complete current engine parity.
  Burst/barrage group targeting and Nechryael encounter context:
  https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Barrage_spells and
  https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Slayer_task/Nechryael . Group DPS is a local estimate
  for identical targets, not a simulation of stacking, movement or Slayer experience rates.
* Equipment skill requirements: OSRSBox contributors, GPL-3.0,
  https://github.com/osrsbox/osrsbox-db/blob/dae12e3400add0c71465f07a334f9d8f86bebce8/data/items/items-skill-requirements.json
  (downloaded September 5, 2026; copied as equipment-requirements.json). This historical
  dataset is incomplete and does not establish all current skill or quest requirements.
  Local requirement corrections allow owned God blessings and Rada's blessings, which
  have no equip-level requirements (acquisition requirements are separate). Blessing
  facts: https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Holy_blessing and
  https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Prayer_items (reviewed September 5, 2026).
* Item, monster and spell facts originate from OSRS Wiki contributors:
  https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/ . Preserve attribution and applicable Wiki notices when
  reusing data. Wiki copyright policy: https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/RuneScape:Copyrights .
* Upstream acknowledges Bitterkoekje's combat spreadsheet and the Wiki calculator contributors.
* Old School RuneScape names and game assets belong to Jagex. This project does not claim

* Gradle wrapper provenance is inherited from the workspace template; see the workspace's
